Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Truly real-time

Messages, typing indicators and read receipts arrive the instant they happen.

Groups that flow

Bring friends together, reply to anything, react, pin what matters.

Private by design

Only people in a conversation can read it. No ads. Block, hide your status, turn off receipts.

Your name, forever

Usernames are unique and permanent, and look-alikes are blocked, so nobody can pose as you.

Pure black & white

A quiet interface in light or dark mode. The only colour is a green dot when friends are online.

Words that arrive alive

New messages gently type themselves onto the screen. Prefer stillness? Turn it off.
